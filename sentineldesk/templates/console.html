How it works

  1. Mask and detect. Emails, phone numbers and account IDs are masked. Language is detected from the text, not from metadata.
  2. Triage. Three calibrated heads predict type, queue and priority.
  3. Precedents. Five similar historical tickets are retrieved, each with its answer.
  4. Gate. Outages go to on-call. Refunds, overrides, low confidence and weak evidence go to a human.
  5. Grounded draft. Every claim cites a precedent and quotes its evidence. A draft that fails validation is withheld.

Nothing is sent to a customer. Every draft is for operator review.
